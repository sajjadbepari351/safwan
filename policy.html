About & Site Policies

How this independent Bangladesh job information directory works.

About Career Radar BD

Career Radar BD is an independent job-information directory intended to help job seekers discover recruitment notices relevant to production, industrial engineering, planning, supply chain, operations, quality, procurement, logistics and related functions in Bangladesh.

We are not an employer, recruitment agency, job board partner, or representative of any company listed on this website. Company names are used only to identify the employer mentioned in a recruitment notice.

Important Disclaimer

  • We do not guarantee that every vacancy in Bangladesh is listed or that every listing remains available at all times.
  • Vacancy details, eligibility, salary, location, benefits and deadlines may change. The employer's original notice or application portal is the authoritative source.
  • Applicants must independently verify the notice, requirements, deadline and application method before applying.
  • We do not collect applications, charge applicants, promise interviews, or guarantee employment.
  • Never send money, passwords, OTPs, banking details or sensitive personal information to an unverified recruiter.
  • Listings marked as unverified leads are discovery results only; they are not confirmed vacancies and should not be treated as active job offers.

A listing being present on Career Radar BD does not mean that the employer endorses this website.

Sources & Attribution

Vacancy information may be identified through publicly accessible employer career pages, recruitment platforms, job notices and news/RSS discovery feeds. Where available, each listing links to the original notice or application page and identifies its source.

We aim to summarize job details in our own words and display factual information such as job title, employer, location, eligibility and application deadline. We do not claim ownership of third-party recruitment notices, trademarks, logos, photographs or other third-party materials.

Copyright & Content Removal Requests

Third-party names, marks and recruitment content remain the property of their respective owners. We do not intend to reproduce complete job advertisements, proprietary images, logos or substantial copyrighted descriptions.

If you are an employer, rights holder or authorized representative and believe that a listing on this website uses content incorrectly, please contact the site maintainer through the GitHub repository's Issues page:

Submit a content correction or removal request ↗

Please include the page/listing URL, the original source, your relationship to the content and the specific material you want reviewed. We will review genuine requests and remove or correct material where appropriate.

Privacy

This website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not currently provide user accounts, job applications, CV uploads, contact forms or a database for collecting visitor profiles.

Search and filter actions run in the visitor's browser. When visitors follow an external link, that third-party website may collect information under its own privacy policy. GitHub Pages and the visitor's browser may process standard technical information such as request logs, as described by their providers.

Do not submit personal, financial or sensitive information to this website. If site functionality changes to collect personal information, this policy should be updated before that collection begins.

Corrections & Updates

We make reasonable efforts to review vacancy details, but errors and expired notices can occur. Please use the original employer or recruitment-platform notice to confirm current information. You may report an incorrect or expired listing through the GitHub Issues link above.
